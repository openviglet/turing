# Demo storefront credits

Tarnfell, its products and its journal are fictional, made for this repository.

## Images

Every product and guide picture is drawn at seed time by [`images.php`](images.php) (GD,
saved as WebP): a flat illustration of each product in its colour, and a fell landscape for
each guide. Nothing is downloaded, and no photograph, real person or real brand's product
appears in any of them. They are part of this repository and dedicated to the public domain
([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)), so they may be
redistributed with it.

[`preview-images.php`](preview-images.php) draws every kind on one contact sheet, to look at
before a seed.

## Narration and music

The demo video's narration (D246, D261) is synthesised speech: ElevenLabs' premade voice
*Matilda* on the `eleven_v3` model, voicing Ana, the invented owner of the store, from the
lines in [`script.json`](../narration/script.json), written for this repository. The developer
walkthrough (D281) is voiced by ElevenLabs' premade voice *Chris*, on the same model, from
[`developer.json`](../narration/developer.json). Both were bought on an ElevenLabs plan that
grants commercial use, so they may be published with the videos.

The music bed is *Monday Market Open*, generated by Alexandre Oliveira with Google Gemini's
music generation (Lyria) and cut by polyweave's `sound.loop` into a 63-bar loop at 120 BPM.
Gemini embeds Google's SynthID watermark in what it generates. Google's terms for Gemini
output do not say plainly whether it may be used commercially, so that is unconfirmed: check
them before publishing the video where commercial terms apply. The ElevenLabs bed it replaced
is kept out of the repository as `music.previous.mp3`.

The audio files are not committed: `npm run story:narration` buys the takes again, and only a
take whose line changed. Each take's `.prov.json` records the voice, the model, the plan and
the line it says; `music.mp3.prov.json` records where the music bed came from.

## Photos

The product and guide photos in [`../photos/`](../photos/) are photorealistic images generated
with Ideogram 4.0 (`npm run demo:photos`, D265), bought through polyweave inside the budget in
`polyweave.toml`. They show the invented products, laid flat on a table, pitched on level grass
or out on the hill, and invented scenes: no real person, place name, brand or logo was asked
for. Every product and guide has one. The repository keeps an 800 px WebP of each, the size a
shop shows, with its `.prov.json` recording the prompt and the service; the full-size PNG
Ideogram returns is not committed. The seed shows each photo whole, over a blurred copy of
itself where its shape differs from the frame, since Ideogram does not always return the aspect
ratio asked. A product or guide without a photo would be drawn by the seed, as above.

## Footage

The demo video's opening and closing play clips of the hills fetched from
[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com) (`npm run demo:broll`, D261), under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), which allows this use
without attribution. Each clip's `.json` in [`../broll/`](../broll/) still names its page and its
author, and the clips themselves are not committed.
