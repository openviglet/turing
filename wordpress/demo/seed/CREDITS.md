# Demo storefront credits

Tarnfell, its products and its journal are fictional, made for this repository.

## Images

Every product and guide picture is drawn at seed time by [`images.php`](images.php) (GD,
saved as WebP): a flat illustration of each product in its colour, and a fell landscape for
each guide. Nothing is downloaded, and no photograph, real person or real brand's product
appears in any of them. They are part of this repository and dedicated to the public domain
([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)), so they may be
redistributed with it.

[`preview-images.php`](preview-images.php) draws every kind on one contact sheet, to look at
before a seed.

## Narration and music

The demo video's narration (D246, D261) is synthesised speech: ElevenLabs' premade voice
*Matilda* on the `eleven_v3` model, voicing Ana, the invented owner of the store, from the
lines in [`script.json`](../narration/script.json), written for this repository. The music
bed under it is a sound generated by ElevenLabs from a text description. Both were bought on an
ElevenLabs plan that grants commercial use, so they may be published with the video.

The audio files are not committed: `npm run story:narration` buys them again, and only a take
whose line changed. Each take's `.prov.json` records the voice, the model, the plan and the
line it says.

## Photos

The product and guide photos in [`../photos/`](../photos/) are photorealistic images generated
with Ideogram 4.0 (`npm run demo:photos`, D265), bought through polyweave inside the budget in
`polyweave.toml`. They show the invented products, laid flat or out on the hill, and invented
scenes: no real person, place name, brand or logo was asked for. The repository keeps an 800 px
WebP of each, the size a shop shows, with its `.prov.json` recording the prompt and the service;
the full-size PNG Ideogram returns is not committed. A product or guide without a photo is drawn
by the seed, as above.
